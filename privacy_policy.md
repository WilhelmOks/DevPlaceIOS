# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

DevPlace does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

## Data collection

The DevPlace app does not collect any data about you. We do not use analytics,
advertising identifiers, tracking SDKs, or any third-party services that would
gather information about you or your device.

## Account information

DevPlace connects to the DevPlace service so you can sign in and use your
account. The email address and password you enter are used solely to
authenticate you with the DevPlace service and are stored only on your device
(in the iOS Keychain). They are never shared with us or any third party for any
other purpose.

## Content you create

Any posts, comments, messages, or other content you choose to submit are sent to
the DevPlace service to provide the app's features, exactly as you would expect
from using the service. The app itself does not collect or retain this content
for any separate purpose.

## Tracking

DevPlace does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new
"Last updated" date.
